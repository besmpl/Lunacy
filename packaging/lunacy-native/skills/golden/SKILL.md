# Lunacy Golden

Treat invocation of `$lunacy-native:golden` with an outcome as explicit selection of the golden workflow for that outcome. If the user gives no task, ask what they want done; do not invent a project or restart a previous goal.

Read and follow the shared [Lunacy native contract](../lunacy/SKILL.md) and canonical [Golden workflow](../lunacy/orchestrator/IMPROVEMENT.md). Apply those references progressively rather than duplicating their rules here. Lunacy can handle a newly authorized, scoped task in an existing repository when current authority and ownership/effect checks permit it; “new” describes the work, not the repository's age. A new task, filename, or installation does not migrate existing or provenance-uncertain work, which retains its original contract and owner.

Before planning dispatch, honor task-scoped coding choices under [Worker model selection](../lunacy/orchestrator/WORKER-MODELS.md). `choose workers` selects coding `bulk` and `judgment` only. Golden has a distinct ADHD selector: `choose ADHD workers`, or the exact form `ADHD workers: gpt-6-astra / low`, binds one model/effort pair for every ADHD generator and deepener; omission keeps `gpt-5.6-luna / high`. Apply the canonical [ADHD worker selection, route, and sealing](../lunacy/orchestrator/IMPROVEMENT.md) rules before divergence. Accept exact choices without redundant questions; ask only for missing or ambiguous choices. These selectors are conversational and do not modify Codex's global model picker.

## Golden execution card

1. Frame the authorized outcome, constraints, preserved behavior, acceptance evidence, and separate release authority.
2. Obtain the task-selected consultation, then run the available ADHD skill with its required isolation, divergence, scoring, trap removal, and focus/convergence using the one sealed ADHD worker pair.
3. Selected ADHD workers own candidate proposals, deepening material, output
repair, and final provocation; Luna/high owns substantive evidence
reconciliation, solution revision, design, and roadmap prose. The parent owns
workflow judgment and acceptance: Astra in its parent role may frame criteria,
select, reject, adopt in the existing `TASK.md`, and accept without authoring
substantive Golden or ADHD-worker output. Astra in an adviser role supplies
bounded advice and evidence only and has no authority to select, reject, adopt,
or accept. An Astra-routed ADHD worker is the selected-worker exception and may
author only its assigned ADHD material.
4. Use the shared orchestrator guidance already linked in this skill for direct adopted-roadmap delivery to the source-owning worker.

The selected ADHD pair—not the later Luna route—authors fresh isolated ADHD generator/deepener proposals, output repair, and final provocation. A new unbound task defaults to `gpt-5.6-luna` / `high`; exact task-local choices remain binding for the entire round. After ADHD, Luna/high authors substantive evidence reconciliation, solution revision, design, and roadmap prose. A mid-round pair change must never mix earlier generators with newly selected deepeners; after settlement, a changed pair requires a newly authorized full ADHD round. Shared routing supplies consultation selection, custody, evidence, cache, acceptance, and finite-stage rules; do not silently fall back after a route starts or replay work whose effects are unknown, while authorized repair after settlement remains allowed. Unknown-effect operations stay behind the shared custody barrier: do not replay, resume, clean up, or replace them. Missing or partial strategy output pauses dependent advancement. Fewer than three viable survivors is an incomplete focus, not a padded winner; malformed output is an output defect, not negative idea evidence. Use the shared finite dispositions `no-worthy-opportunity`, `unresolved-evidence`, and `review-deferred` truthfully. Follow the shared rejection handoff for local Luna repair versus a newly authorized full ADHD round when a causal mechanism fails.

Source-only and design-only authority delivers only its authorized artifacts. Ordinary Lunacy defaults remain unchanged, and this shortcut does not alter global/model configuration, create a scheduler or ledger, grant publication permission, or invent a task. Explicit Golden selection is required for this workflow; a task-free invocation asks what the user wants rather than restarting prior work.
