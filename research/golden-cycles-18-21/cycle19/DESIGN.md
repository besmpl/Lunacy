# Cycle 19 source-grounded design

## Decision

**No product or workflow guidance change is supported by the current evidence.**
Remain idle for this improvement vector and keep the accepted cycle-18 source
unchanged. This is a truthful `no-worthy-opportunity` disposition for the
accessible cycle-19 candidate set, not a claim that every possible improvement
was searched and not a measured claim about cost or savings.

The current source already has two materially different paths:

* Explicit Golden work runs consultation -> actual ADHD -> Luna/high source-checked
  design -> parent adoption -> coherent delivery -> independent acceptance
  (`orchestrator/IMPROVEMENT.md:93-99`).
* An ordinary known-root-cause fix can use direct Lunacy without those strategy
  stages (`orchestrator/IMPROVEMENT.md:297-305`).

Cycle 19 is itself explicitly under the full Golden contract, so none of the
selected proposals may waive a stage in this cycle. A future guidance change
would require a new parent adoption; this design grants no source, release,
installation, or implementation authority.

“Marginal contribution” here means an attributable decision change, material
risk caught, or necessary acceptance finding. It is not inferred from record
length, line count, model-call count, or absence of a cited contribution.

## Candidate reconciliation

### assumption-5 — evidence-triggered advice

This candidate would suppress the mandatory consultation when a local scan
looks routine and summon it later on a failure seam. The source does not define
such an admission predicate: `IMPROVEMENT.md:94` makes consultation precede
ADHD, while `:297-299` distinguishes Golden from ordinary Lunacy. The
focus proposal's historical “no-op” labels cannot prove the absent-adviser
counterfactual, and its offline replay is future evidence rather than a current
waiver. Adding a trigger would be a new policy boundary whose false negatives
could suppress cross-cutting judgment; no net simplification is evidenced.
Reject for this cycle.

### markets-6 — risk-band standing orders

The useful kernel—reuse stable evidence for recurring shapes—is already
supported when the same cycle's evidence and premises are unchanged
(`IMPROVEMENT.md:299`) and by progressive reads/compact TASK memory
(`IMPROVEMENT.md:101-113`). A standing-order risk band would add durable
classification, expiry/repricing semantics, or a dynamic router, despite the
source explicitly rejecting a dashboard, cache, ledger, launcher, and dynamic
router (`:307`) and the uniform record contract rejecting duplicate state
(`WORKSPACE.md:13-21`). No recurring shape or comparative outcome is in the
named records. Reject; do not turn a market metaphor into stateful machinery.

### assumption-1 — decision-boundary slices

The source already allows multiple coherent implementation slices only after
one adopted outcome and requires exact assignments and independent acceptance
(`IMPROVEMENT.md:96-99`; `WORKSPACE.md:23,58-62`). The focus proposal's
slice manifest and parent merge would add representation and parent-authored
substantive synthesis, conflicting with the source authorship boundary
(`IMPROVEMENT.md:11-22`) and the required one-seed focus packet
(`:95`). Coupled slices also require discovering dependencies, which can
erase the proposed saving. Existing compact TASK and evidence-reuse rules are
the lower-cost supported seam. Reject absent a naturally occurring attributable
case.

## Outcome and bounded revisit

No implementation slice is selected. Do not edit
`cycle18/implementation-checkout`, package files, tests, scripts, manifests,
or records outside `cycle19/ADHD.md`, `cycle19/DESIGN.md`, this report, and
necessary design logs.

Reopen this vector only under a new parent adoption when one naturally
occurring, current-source outcome has a retrievable bounded record set naming
the task/adoption/assignment anchors and showing all of the following:

1. The applicable current route and evidence-reuse rules were followed.
2. A stage's output was available and its specific contribution (decision
   change, material risk caught, or necessary acceptance finding) is identified,
   rather than classified from a label or stage count.
3. A distinct parent reconstruction, repeated read, or repair was observed
   with evidence that it changed no adopted outcome and was attributable to
   that stage—not merely to ordinary implementation repair or acceptance.
4. The same case records preserved exact routing, worker authorship, source
   custody, required checks, independent acceptance, and separate release
   authority.
5. A smallest candidate clarification can be stated without a new runtime,
   ledger, schema, mandatory template, dynamic router, acceptance waiver, or
   parent-authored worker solution.

The future record may use existing TASK/report/log artifacts; it must not become
a new measurement service. If these facts are absent or contradictory, retain
`unresolved-evidence` and remain idle. If a new candidate is eventually
adopted, the parent must specify its exact source path, owner, literal route,
fresh context, permitted effects, bounded checks, stop/recovery, and acceptance
in a new adoption before any source work. A future trial, if authorized, must
be a bounded comparison of naturally occurring outcomes, not a retrospective
claim extracted from this cycle.
