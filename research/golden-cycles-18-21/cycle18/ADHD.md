seeds. A score is prioritization evidence, not adoption or proof of savings.

## Selection and focus material

The fixed weighted top three non-traps are:

1. **child-2** — 8.55, literal: distinguish source/consumer contract shape
   from descriptive prose while retaining behavioral proof.
2. **regulator-2** — 7.55, method: declare an uncovered behavior and expected
   counterexample for each helper.
3. **child-4** — 7.55, repair: identify the first unsupported source → behavior
   → report arrow before repair rather than launch another checker.

Raw focus evidence is retained at `FOCUS-1.json`, `FOCUS-2.json`, and
`FOCUS-3.json` (the parent dispatch supplied only its selected seed to each):

* **FOCUS-1 / child-2:** use only three guidance terms—contract literal,
  structural behavior, descriptive prose. Preserve exact routes, keys,
  serialized tokens, JSON paths, and consumer-visible errors; use outcome or
  structural checks for descriptive prose. First step was a source-backed
  table of each of the nine attempts’ literals, owner/consumer evidence, and
  required exactness, then independent classification. Its risk is hiding a
  contract break through misclassification. It proposed replaying the
  existing corpus, but no available corpus or causal replay entitlement is
  established here.
* **FOCUS-2 / regulator-2:** its connection is a two-line helper declaration
  of uncovered behavior plus smallest observable counterexample, with one
  acceptance spot-check per helper class. This is useful diagnostic material,
  but mandatory declarations and spot-checks add ceremony and do not follow
  from the source contract; they are not adopted.
* **FOCUS-3 / child-4:** its sketch is a source → behavior → report
  “first-broken-arrow” triage recipe. It correctly preserves exact routes,
  required tests, independent acceptance, and no new checker; however it
  assumes the six failures were required checks and that automatic same-check
  reruns were allowed. Those assumptions contradict this assignment’s real
  attempt limits and are rejected. Its worker-authored wildcard was:
  **“What if checkers were allowed to emit only an evidence pointer to
  source, behavior, or report—not a retry recommendation—so every next action
  must remain tied to the actual broken arrow?”**

The reconciled direction is therefore one compact, non-mandatory method
clarification combining child-2’s contract-shape boundary with child-4’s
diagnostic ordering. It does not require regulator-2’s declaration/ledger,
new checker, report-validation pass, automatic replay, or a general prose
exemption. Parent findings also correct the incident description: there were
nine attempts and six failed harness runs (two artifact self-review and four
final-review logs), not nine failed scripts or six independent incidents.
