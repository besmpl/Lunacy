# Plan — discover the next four truly major wins

## Goal

Identify and rank exactly four genuinely major next directions for Lunacy after the complete READY candidate in `Lunacy/runs/implement-next-big-wins-v2/phases/p5/hard-gate-01.md`.

## Baseline

- Current working candidate: baseline commit `9e77159577a5fc8d0fb5e3f182c8fdd8584df4b7` plus the fully integrated uncommitted P1–P4 implementation certified by P5.
- Already delivered/implemented and therefore not candidates: lifecycle controller, segmented v1/checkpoints, dispatch coordinator, recovery forensics, multi-run fleet coordinator, decision inbox/exact promotion, segmented/v2 reader/oracle/private experiment, and resumable outer release envelope.
- Existing conditional boundaries remain facts: no default/managed v2 writer value claim; kernels/release transactions remain authority; additive routes are private/explicit.

## Major-win bar

A direction qualifies only if it can materially change Lunacy's autonomy, usefulness, scale, reliability, developer/operator experience, or measurable efficiency. Cleanup, refactoring, dashboards without control impact, minor CLI polish, telemetry alone, or an unproven optimization do not qualify.

Every candidate must include exact current evidence, fact versus inference, why it is large, the smallest coherent first release, dependencies/ordering, principal risks, and decisive proof. Do not invent performance/token/provider claims.

## Method

P1: four independent Luna/xhigh read-only scouts across complementary domains. Each ranks at most three candidates and explicitly rejects smaller ideas.

P2: one bound exact Sol/high worker synthesizes the settled reports into exactly four mutually distinct directions. It must merge duplicates rather than select one per scout.

Parent final gate spot-checks exact cited seams and presents the ranked four. Discovery only: no product/release/install/commit/push changes.
